## SECTION 4 — CASE STUDIES
Header: CASE STUDIES
Intro: Three different categories, one repeatable system: capture high-intent search at scale, then turn it into tracked pipeline.
Three cards, identical layout, each with: banner image, eyebrow (CASE STUDY · category), title, "The challenge," "What we built," three cyan stat chips, a highlighted "Why it matters for Lob" box, and a "Read the full study →" link.

**Card 1 — Omi**
- Eyebrow: CASE STUDY · B2B / TECHNICAL SAAS
- Title: Programmatic SEO for a hard-to-reach technical audience
- The challenge: A niche AI-wearable brand needed visibility with developers and power users in a fast-moving, crowded category — an audience that ignores generic marketing.
- What we built: A programmatic SEO engine with deep intent mapping across thousands of technical, long-tail queries — a dual-layer strategy capturing both discovery and conversion-ready searches.
- Stat chips: 204K+ organic clicks · 7M+ impressions · 3,900+ pages ranking
- Why it matters for Lob: The closest analog to your business — a technical B2B audience and long-tail, persona-specific search captured at scale. Exactly the opportunity you flagged.
- Link: https://www.ambitiouslabs.io/case-study/omi

**Card 2 — Curex**
- Eyebrow: CASE STUDY · HEALTHCARE / TELEHEALTH
- Title: Replacing expensive paid search with an organic engine
- The challenge: Paid search costs ($4–$7+ per click) were becoming unsustainable as the company scaled in one of the most competitive search categories online.
- What we built: An AI-powered programmatic SEO system generating thousands of intent-mapped pages across conditions, treatments, and location-specific demand.
- Stat chips: 1M+ organic clicks · 91% of traffic from the new system · $4M–$7M est. equivalent ad value
- Why it matters for Lob: Proof we build organic into infrastructure that lowers dependence on paid spend — the same compounding layer we'd build under your program.
- Link: https://www.ambitiouslabs.io/case-study/curex

**Card 3 — Anchorpest**
- Eyebrow: CASE STUDY · SERVICE / LEAD GEN
- Title: From traffic to tracked, booked leads
- The challenge: Near-total dependence on a single Google Ads campaign, almost no organic visibility, and larger competitors dominating search.
- What we built: A rebuilt acquisition engine — search behavior mapped into topic clusters, 176+ programmatic pages, technical SEO fixes, and full lead-tracking infrastructure to measure calls and form fills.
- Stat chips: +940% organic traffic (<8 mo) · ~10% organic conversion rate · 21–25 inbound leads/week
- Why it matters for Lob: The full loop you're after — content engine + technical SEO + conversion tracking, with leads as the outcome, not vanity traffic. Maps directly to the CRO + measurement scope.
- Link: https://www.ambitiouslabs.io/case-study/anchorpest
